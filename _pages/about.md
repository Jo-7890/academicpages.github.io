    <p class="intro-eyebrow">HUMAN FACTORS &nbsp;·&nbsp; HUMAN–AI INTERACTION &nbsp;·&nbsp; HEALTHCARE</p>
    <p>I am <strong>Yu Jiang</strong>, a second-year PhD student in the <a href="https://www.dase.hku.hk/">Department of Data and Systems Engineering</a> at <a href="https://www.hku.hk/">The University of Hong Kong</a>, advised by <a href="https://www.dase.hku.hk/people/c-k-l-or">Prof. Calvin K. L. Or</a>.</p>
    <p>My research lies at the intersection of <strong>human–computer interaction, human factors, and healthcare</strong>. I study how people evaluate information, make decisions, and interact with intelligent systems, with an interest in designing technologies that better support human judgment.</p>
    <p class="intro-background">Previously, I studied Human Factors Engineering and User Experience at East China Normal University and Applied Psychology at Zhejiang University.</p>
  </div>

  <h2>Research Interests</h2>
  <p class="section-lead">My work connects cognitive and behavioral research with the design of human-centered technologies.</p>
  <div class="research-card-grid">
    <div class="research-interest-card"><span class="interest-number">01</span><h3>Human–AI Interaction</h3><p>How people understand, evaluate, and appropriately rely on AI-generated information and recommendations.</p></div>
    <div class="research-interest-card"><span class="interest-number">02</span><h3>Healthcare Decision-Making</h3><p>Information evaluation, individual differences, and decision support in health-related contexts.</p></div>
    <div class="research-interest-card"><span class="interest-number">03</span><h3>Human Factors &amp; Cognition</h3><p>Cognitive and behavioral processes that inform usable, trustworthy interactive systems.</p></div>
